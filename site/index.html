What is KTV PRO K500?

A karaoke processor built around the singer—not a general-purpose mixer with echo added.

K500 sits between your music sources, microphones and speaker system. Its DSP handles the functions that make karaoke easier and more enjoyable: music key control, vocal ambience, feedback management, tonal correction, dynamics, crossover and room output distribution.

Karaoke-specific sound features

Functions that change the singing experience—not only the meter levels.

The controls below are mapped in the K500 preset and live-control model used by SONKUPIK STUDIO.

01 · MUSIC KEY

Lower or raise the song to fit the singer

The accompaniment can be shifted from -7 to +7 semitones. A song that feels too high or too low becomes easier to perform without searching for another backing track.

♭7♭40♯4♯7
This is music transposition, not automatic vocal Auto-Tune.
02 · VOCAL SPACE

Studio-style reverb and echo, tuned separately

Reverb includes level, decay, pre-delay and tone filtering. Echo includes level, repeat, delay and tone filtering. Both also have dedicated parametric EQ sections.

Pre-delayDecayDelayRepeatHPF / LPF5-band PEQ
The result can be tight and intimate, wide and luxurious, or rhythmic—without relying on one generic echo knob.
03 · VOCAL CONTROL

Make microphones clearer, steadier and safer

Mic A and Mic B each have 10-band parametric EQ. The microphone path also includes HPF/LPF, noise gate, compressor and shared FBX depth for feedback-control tuning.

Gate→10-band PEQ→Compressor→FBX
04 · MUSIC CONTROL

Give backing tracks weight without covering the voice

Music has source-specific gain, key control, 7-band parametric EQ, HPF/LPF and fast Bass, Mid, Mid Frequency and Treble controls.

Whole-room DSP

Control what reaches every speaker zone.

K500 is designed beyond a stereo master output. It exposes dedicated paths for Main L/R, Surround L/R, Center and Subwoofer, with channel-specific processing and routing.

MAIN L / R

Primary listening image

Independent left/right level, mic, music, reverb and echo mix, compressor and 7-band PEQ.

SURROUND L / R

Room depth and timing

Independent level and mix, compressor, 5-band PEQ and separate left/right delay alignment.

CENTER

Focused vocal support

Dedicated output level, mic/music/effect balance, compressor and 5-band PEQ.

SUBWOOFER

Controlled low-frequency impact

Dedicated mix and level, compressor, HPF/LPF crossover control and 5-band PEQ.

Product clarity

Frequently asked questions

Is the ±7 key control the same as Auto-Tune?

No. It transposes the music accompaniment up or down to suit the singer's range. It does not automatically correct the pitch of the microphone voice.

Why are the vocal effects described as professional-style?

Because reverb and echo are separate engines with timing, amount, filtering and dedicated EQ controls—the same categories engineers use to build vocal ambience. No claim is made that K500 duplicates a specific premium brand, artist or studio chain.

Will it work with every karaoke processor?

No universal compatibility is implied. SONKUPIK STUDIO targets compatible KTV PRO K500-family workflows; device revision, firmware, connection method and Windows configuration can affect behavior.

Which Windows package should I use?

Use Setup for normal daily operation and faster startup. Use Portable when installation is not possible or a self-contained copy is required.

Does the website upload audio or include tracking?

The website does not process audio and contains no advertising or analytics code. It contacts GitHub only to resolve current official release information.
